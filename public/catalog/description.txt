11. Fabric-Backed Vinyl Wallcoverings
Zen wall fabric backed PDF
Based on the provided PDF, the wallpapers in this catalog belong to the category of Fabric-Backed Vinyl Wallcoverings.
This category is distinct from standard "Korean wallpaper" (which is typically paper-backed), "interior film" (which is usually self-adhesive PVC), or "mural wallpaper" (which often features a single large-scale image).

Catalog Description and Key Features
The Zen Walls collection by the brand Durawall is a professional-grade wallcovering designed for both commercial and residential use.

Composition: These wallcoverings feature a vinyl surface with a poly-cotton fabric backing. This construction makes them significantly more durable and heavier than standard wallpaper, weighing approximately 15 oz/ly (340g/m2).

Safety and Durability: They are fire-retardant (Class A / Class B1) and resistant to water and scratches. They are also washable, allowing dirt to be wiped off easily.

Health and Environment: The products are made from low-VOC, phthalate-free, and antimicrobial materials, printed with water-based inks.

Micro-Venting Technology: A unique feature of this catalog is the option for "micro-venting". This process adds thousands of invisible tiny holes to the material to allow for breathability, which prevents moisture from being trapped and reduces the risk of mold and mildew.

Installation: Due to their weight and fabric backing, they require a heavy-duty adhesive like the recommended Bartoline Ready Mix Wallcovering Adhesive. They are designed for a "reverse hang" and "random match" installation.
